# Research Plan
### Research Objectives
**Feature Scope:** Understand user preferences for how the flagging/favoriting feature should function: agency-wide vs. individual basis.
**Design Preferences:** Identify user expectations for the visual design, placement, and interaction model of the flagging/favoriting UI.
**Workflow Integration:** Explore specific scenarios and use cases where users would benefit from flagging/favoriting functionality.

### Research Questions
- Should flags or favorites be accessible by all users within an agency, or should they be private to individual users?
- What type of UI elements and layouts are most intuitive and useful for displaying flagged/favorited items?
- How does the flagging/favoriting feature fit into users' existing workflows and tasks in FDMS?

### Participants
10-15 federal employees who actively use the Federal Docket Management System for managing rulemakings, dockets, or public comments. 
These participants have been pre-identified and represent a subset of docket managers and staff.

## Research Methodology
### Survey Design:
- Utilize Optimal Workshop to create a questionnaire that captures both qualitative and quantitative data.
- Focus questions on preferences, use cases, and design expectations specific to the flagging/favoriting feature.
### Data Collection:
- Distribute the survey via email or FDMS internal communication channels.
- Allow one week for participants to complete the survey, with a mid-week reminder.
### Analysis:
- Quantify responses to preference-based questions (e.g., agency-wide vs. individual use, design options).
- Conduct thematic analysis of open-ended responses to identify common use cases and pain points.

## Deliverables
### A report summarizing key findings, including:
- Preferences for agency-wide vs. individual flagging.
- User expectations for UI design and placement.
- Examples of specific workflows that benefit from the feature.
- Design recommendations for the flagging/favoriting feature, including:
- The preferred functionality (visibility and scope).
- Suggested UI elements and interaction patterns.
- Suggested implementation roadmap based on perceived value and user needs.
