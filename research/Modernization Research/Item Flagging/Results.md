# FDMS Item Flagging Research Spike Results

## Hypotheses Tested (Tl;DR)

Do agency partners want a restored item flagging and favoriting feature set (as in FDMS 3 and 4) in the modernized system?

## Participants

- **FDMS Concepts**: 6 regulatory agency staff (of various agency size) from our eRulemaking Innovation Team

## We move to a hybrid approach that folds the concepts of flagging and favoriting into a "tagging" system

- Participants indicated that they liked both the flagging and favoriting features of the previous systems, but that they only used it for personal organization rather than agency wide coordination
- Using icons instead of modern enterprise filtering and tagging indicators is a legacy behavior and not a current best practice, we should look to "competitors" for inspiration
- Users should have tags on items in the system that are primarily visible to themselves, but higher permissioned users can elevate a tag on an item to a "global" visibility status

## Why it matters:

Users enjoyed the flagging and favoriting feature set in previous versions of FDMS, but it was never fully baked. We don't want to recreate this old paradigm, instead we want to elevate it to a modern system of tags and filters. This should help users more quickly locate, organize, and visualize the complex web of documents and information they are handling on a daily basis.
